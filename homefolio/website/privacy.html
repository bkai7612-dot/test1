Homefolio helps you keep information about your home in one place. This policy explains what personal information we hold, why, and your rights. We've written it to be read, not skimmed past.

Who we are

Homefolio is provided by [your company or trading name], [registered address], company number [number] ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the information in your account. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, registration number [ICO number]. Contact us at privacy@homefolio.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, the first name you give us, and your password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Your home information: whatever you choose to add, such as property addresses, rooms, appliances, warranties, maintenance tasks, utility and insurance details, meter readings, household members and contacts.
  • Files: photos and documents you upload.
  • Plan and purchases: whether you have Homefolio Plus and when it renews. Payments are handled by Apple, Google or Stripe; we never see your card details.
  • Technical information: basic logs needed to run and secure the service (for example, sign-in times and error reports).

Please only add information about other people (such as household members) if you have their agreement.

How we use it

  • To provide Homefolio: store your information, show it to you on your devices, and send reminders you've asked for.
  • To send essential emails, such as confirming your account or resetting your password.
  • To manage Homefolio Plus.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

Our legal basis is performance of our contract with you (providing the service you signed up for), and our legitimate interest in keeping Homefolio secure. We do not use your information for marketing without your consent.

Sponsored cards

Free accounts may see one small card labelled "Sponsored" on some pages. Which card appears depends only on the page you're on, never on your personal information. We count how many times each card is shown and clicked as anonymous daily totals, with no link to you or your device. Advertisers receive only those totals. We don't use advertising networks or tracking, and we never share or sell your information to advertisers. Plus accounts see no sponsored cards.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We use these trusted service providers to run Homefolio, each under a data processing agreement:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in London, UK.
  • Vercel: hosting of our website and web app.
  • [your email provider, e.g. Resend]: sending account emails.
  • RevenueCat, Apple, Google and Stripe: processing Plus purchases (only if you buy Plus).

Some providers may process limited data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards approved under UK law, such as the International Data Transfer Agreement.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your information and files are deleted straight away from our live systems, and from backups within [7] days.

Keeping it safe

Your information is only visible to your account, enforced by our database's access rules. Files are kept in private storage and shared with your devices through short-lived links. All connections are encrypted.

Cookies and local storage

Homefolio uses your device's storage only to keep you signed in and remember preferences such as dark mode. These are strictly necessary, so no cookie banner is needed. Our website uses [cookie-free analytics, e.g. Vercel Web Analytics / no analytics].

Your rights

You can access, correct, download (Settings → Your data) and delete (Settings → Delete account) your information at any time in the app. You also have the right to object to or restrict how we use it. Email privacy@homefolio.co.uk and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

Children

Homefolio is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we make important changes we'll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
