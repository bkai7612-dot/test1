Support

Email support@homefolio.co.uk and we'll reply within two working days.

Common questions

I've forgotten my password

On the sign-in screen choose Forgotten your password?, enter your email, and follow the link we send. Then sign in to the app with your new password.

I didn't get the confirmation or reset email

Check your spam or junk folder for an email from hello@homefolio.co.uk. It can take a few minutes. Still nothing? Email support and we'll help.

How do I install the Windows app?

See Download → Windows. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway.

Does my information sync between devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same email on your phone, PC and browser and you'll see the same homes and documents.

How do I get my data out?

In Homefolio go to Settings → Your data → Download. You get a file with everything you've entered. Uploaded documents can be downloaded from the Documents page.

How do I cancel Homefolio Plus?

See Homefolio Plus. You keep Plus until the end of the period you've paid for.

Delete your account

You can delete your account and all of its information, documents and photos at any time:

  1. Open Homefolio and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Delete account and choose Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm. Everything is permanently removed straight away.

Can't sign in? Email support@homefolio.co.uk from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account" and we'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you pay for Plus through the App Store or Google Play, cancel the subscription there too, as deleting your account doesn't stop store billing.
