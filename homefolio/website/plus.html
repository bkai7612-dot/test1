Questions

Can I switch devices?

Yes. Plus is linked to your Homefolio account, so it works on every device you sign in on, whichever one you bought it on.

How do I cancel?

If you subscribed on iPhone, cancel in the App Store settings. On Android, in Google Play → Subscriptions. On the web, in Homefolio → Settings → Your plan → Manage. You keep Plus until the end of the period you've paid for.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. You can still see everything. You just can't add another property or more files beyond the free limits.
